### 3. Fractional Real Estate Trust Infrastructure

**The deliverable:** A complete evidence vault for a property portfolio offering fractional shares to global investors.

For developers selling fractional shares, investor friction is the biggest barrier to global capital. An investor in Mumbai or London cannot physically verify the asset. The Handshake solves this.

I build the trust infrastructure for the offering:

- Per-property `claims.json` with maintenance, tax, occupancy, and condition claims
- Text Bridge for all supporting PDFs (permits, invoices, inspection reports)
- PGP-signed manifest — AI bots can verify the rent roll and roof permits automatically
- `external_verification` links to county assessor and permit records

> *"Everyone is racing to put property deeds on the blockchain — but who is putting the new roof receipt in claims.json?"*

**Best for:** Real estate developers, fractional ownership platforms, property syndicators seeking global investor audiences.
