READ ME FIRST – Super Simple Instructions

Hi! Welcome to the Grok QuickStart.

You do NOT need to know how to code.

Here’s exactly what to do (takes about 10–15 minutes to get the files, then a few more minutes with Grok’s help):

1. Download this entire "quickstart-grok" folder.
2. Gather your resume + ALL your evidence files (PDFs + .txt sidecars).
3. Upload the whole folder + your resume to Grok.
4. Paste the prompt inside 2-GROK_QUICKSTART_Machine.md.
5. Grok will return two files: claims.json and resume.html.
6. Put your evidence files into the "evidence" folder.
7. Reply to Grok with the words “next step”.

Grok will then guide you one command at a time through PGP setup and Wrangler/Cloudflare deployment.

That’s it.

Your verifiable resume vault is now ready.

Questions? Just keep chatting with Grok and say “next step” whenever you’re ready for the next instruction.
